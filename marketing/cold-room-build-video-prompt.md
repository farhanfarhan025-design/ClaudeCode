# Cold Room Build: AI Video Prompt (scene by scene)

**Concept:** A 4-person team from The New Doha Kitchen Equipment Services W.L.L. builds a walk-in cold storage room inside a commercial kitchen in Doha, Qatar, from an empty space to a working cold room.
**Length:** about 55 seconds (9 scenes)
**Format:** 16:9 for YouTube/website, or 9:16 for Reels/TikTok/Shorts
**Works with:** Sora, Google Veo, Kling, Runway, Hailuo, Pika. Generate each scene as its own clip, then join them in CapCut or Premiere.

---

## Global style block (paste at the start of every scene prompt)

> Cinematic commercial video, photorealistic, 4K, shallow depth of field, smooth gimbal camera movement, bright clean lighting, cool blue and white colour grade with warm highlights. Setting: a modern stainless-steel commercial kitchen in Doha, Qatar. The crew is 4 South Asian and Arab technicians in matching navy-blue work uniforms with reflective strips, white safety helmets, safety gloves and safety shoes. The walk-in cold room uses white insulated PU sandwich panels with cam-lock joints, a stainless-steel hinged door and aluminium corner profiles. Professional, confident, skilled teamwork. No text, no distorted faces, no extra fingers.

**The same 4 crew members in every scene:**
1. **Supervisor**: early 40s, short beard, carries a tablet and a measuring tape
2. **Technician A**: tall, handles the panels
3. **Technician B**: slim, uses the drill and cam-lock key
4. **Refrigeration technician**: glasses, works on the condensing unit and wiring

---

## Scene 1: Opening, the empty space (0:00–0:05)
> Wide establishing shot of an empty corner of a large commercial kitchen in Doha. Morning sunlight comes through high windows, stainless-steel counters line the walls, and the floor is clean. The four technicians walk into frame from the left carrying toolboxes, and the supervisor unrolls a floor plan. Slow dolly forward.

**On-screen text (add in editing):** *"From empty space…"*

## Scene 2: Measuring and marking (0:05–0:11)
> Close-up of the supervisor's hands pulling a yellow measuring tape across the floor. Cut to a technician marking chalk lines, then a green laser level beam across the floor and wall. Low-angle shot, focused expressions, crisp detail.

## Scene 3: Floor panels (0:11–0:17)
> Two technicians lay thick white insulated floor panels onto the marked area and press them into place. Technician B kneels and turns a cam-lock key to join the panels. Overhead top-down shot, then a slow orbit.

## Scene 4: Raising the walls (0:17–0:25)
> Technicians A and B lift a tall white insulated wall panel together and stand it upright on the floor panels. The supervisor guides it into alignment. The cold room walls go up one by one in a time-lapse. Wide shot, camera slowly rising.

**On-screen text:** *"Premium PU insulated panels"*

## Scene 5: Ceiling and corner profiles (0:25–0:31)
> A technician on an aluminium ladder slides a ceiling panel into place from above. Close-up of a cordless drill fixing an aluminium corner profile and a silicone gun sealing a joint cleanly. Detailed macro shots, satisfying precision.

## Scene 6: Installing the door (0:31–0:37)
> Two technicians mount a heavy stainless-steel cold room door onto its hinges. The supervisor opens and closes it, and it shuts with a firm airtight seal. Close-up of the rubber gasket pressing and the chrome handle locking. Medium shot, slight slow motion.

## Scene 7: Refrigeration unit (0:37–0:44)
> The refrigeration technician mounts an evaporator unit on the inside ceiling, then outside connects copper refrigerant pipes to a condensing unit. Close-up of a brazing flame on copper pipe with sparks, then pressure gauges on a manifold. Cut to a digital temperature controller being wired into a control panel.

**On-screen text:** *"Energy-efficient refrigeration"*

## Scene 8: Power on and cooling (0:44–0:50)
> The supervisor presses the start button on the control panel. The evaporator fans spin up and the digital display counts down from 25°C to 2°C in a time-lapse. Cold mist and frost form inside, cool blue lighting fills the room. Dramatic close-up of the display, then a pull-back.

## Scene 9: The final result (0:50–0:58)
> The four technicians stand proudly in front of the finished white cold room, and the supervisor gives a thumbs-up. Kitchen chefs in white uniforms start stocking the shelves inside with fresh vegetables, meat trays and crates. Slow cinematic pull-back through the open door, which then closes. Fade to white.

## Audio suggestions
- **Music:** upbeat corporate or inspirational track that builds up (search "corporate motivational" on CapCut, Pixabay or Epidemic Sound)
- **Sound effects:** drill whirr, panel thud, cam-lock click, door seal "thunk", compressor hum, a cold "whoosh" at power-on
- **Optional voice-over (about 25 words):**
  *"At The New Doha Kitchen, we design, build and install cold storage rooms, built right, sealed tight, and ready to keep your kitchen fresh. Call us today."*

## Tips
- Keep the **global style block** in every prompt so the crew and uniforms stay consistent from clip to clip.
- AI video tools misspell words, so **don't ask the AI to write your company name**. Add the logo and text in editing.
- Most tools make 5–10 second clips. Generate 2–3 versions of each scene and keep the best one.
- For a vertical (9:16) version, add *"vertical 9:16 framing, subject centred"* to the style block.
