# The team

Names must match `scorecard/roster.json` exactly: they are how calls are credited.

## Stage 1: desks

Each desk: analysts file first, then the lead writes 2–3 sentences on what the desk concludes, where its analysts
disagree, and which calls the lead backs.

**Market News Research Lead**: the day's news flow and how the market took it
- Research Analyst: pulls the relevant filings and news from the brief
- Sentiment Analyst: reads the tone of the coverage
- Stock Movement Analyst: explains the big price moves, or says the brief doesn't explain them
- News Credibility Analyst: separates official disclosures (exchange filings) from reports and rumour
- Market Impact Analyst: judges what each story means for price relative to its size

**Equity Research Lead**: company fundamentals
- Fundamental Research Analyst: earnings, balance sheet, valuation, as far as the brief shows them
- Financial Forensics Analyst: accounting and governance red flags
- Pre-Earnings Intelligence Analyst: what's expected before upcoming results

**Head of Industry & Geopolitical Research**: sector structure and policy
- Industry Research Analyst: sector structure, order books, competition
- Geopolitical Risk Analyst: policy, defence, trade and global events

**Head of Market Activity & Ownership Research**: trading activity and who owns what
- Market Volume Intelligence Analyst: volumes and unusual activity
- Insider Activity Analyst: promoter and insider trades, pledges
- Fund Holdings Research Analyst: mutual fund and FII holding changes, block and bulk deals
- Short-Seller Research Analyst: short interest and short reports

## Stage 2: review desk (reads all desk filings)

**Independent Research Review Lead**: rules which desk calls survive challenge
- Bull Case Analyst: the strongest case for the names the desks called
- Bear Case Analyst: the strongest case against
- Research Validation Analyst: checks every desk claim against the brief and names unsupported ones and who made them

## Stage 3: Chief

**Chief Market Intelligence Officer** writes the close-of-day:

```
Close-of-day — <date>
Chief: CMIO · pack TEAM-<YYYYMMDD>
Analysis only — not investment advice.

Holds (facts):     one bullet per sourced fact, HIGH/MED/LOW on the source
Read:              what it means, citing the desk
Contested:         where desks or the review disagree
Gaps:              what the team could not see
```

then the CALL lines for every role's calls, the Chief's own included.
