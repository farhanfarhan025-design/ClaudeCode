## 2. What we sell

### Cold rooms (main offer) [OK to say]

| Room | Temperature | Panel |
|---|---|---|
| Chiller | +2°C to +8°C | 80–100 mm PUF |
| Freezer | −18°C to −22°C | 100–150 mm PUF |

A turnkey room includes: insulated panels, door, refrigeration system, control panel,
lighting, sealing, testing, commissioning, handover with operation training and
as-built documentation.

### Maintenance [OK to say]

We service and repair cold rooms and commercial refrigeration in Doha. Done today for
bakeries, farms and restaurants (Al Noor Bakery, BSI, Ruwais Farm, Lean N Fit).
**Annual maintenance contracts (AMC) are a goal, not yet an established product**: no AMC
is contracted today. Say "scheduled maintenance" or "a service plan"; don't claim existing
AMC clients.

### Freezer / chiller unit replacement [OK to say]

Replacing refrigeration units on an existing room (Lean N Fit freezer unit replacement).

### Out of scope [OK to say, when asked]

Civil works, foundations, main power supply from the MDB, structural steel, BMS/IoT
integration (quoted separately on request). Blast freezing, ripening and pharma rooms
need Farhan's review before we claim we do them.

## 4. What makes us different [OK to say]

Use **one** of these per email, never a list.

1. **Designed for Doha summers.** Every system is sized at **+45°C ambient** with at least
   a **20% capacity margin**, running no more than 16 hours a day. Rooms hold temperature in
   August, not just in the showroom.
2. **One contractor end to end.** Survey, design, supply, install, commission, train and
   maintain. One phone number when something goes wrong.
3. **Fast for a fit-out.** About **4–5 weeks from LPO** to handover on a ready site:
   materials 3–4 weeks, installation 5–7 working days, commissioning 1–2 days.
4. **Site survey first.** Final dimensions are confirmed on site before anything is
   fabricated.
5. **Food-safe detailing.** Food-grade skins, gaskets and sealant, sanitary coving and an
   anti-trap door release as standard.
6. **Local and here after handover.** Doha team that already services bakeries,
   restaurants and farms.
