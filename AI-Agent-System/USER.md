## Preferred communication

- **Direct and numeric.** Lead with the number and the decision needed.
- **Flag, don't bury.** Discrepancies, shortfalls and mismatches go at the top, not in a footnote.
- **Don't re-ask what's already been said.** Extract from context; ask only for genuinely
  missing values. (This is an explicit standing instruction in his existing skills.)
- **Never guess a financial figure.** A one-line clarifying question beats a wrong contract total.

## Decision preferences

- Present options with the recommendation first and the number attached.
- Show the cost of the alternative, not just the merits of the choice.
- He will override a rule when a deal demands it — that is his right as owner.
  The system's job is to make sure the override is **conscious and logged**, never accidental.

## Never assume

- **Never assume a tax or VAT line applies.** See `RULES.md` — this is absolute on invoices.
- Never assume quotation payment terms survived into the contract — **LPO/LOA terms govern**.
- Never assume a job has an LPO. Several are verbal or cash.
- Never assume the register is current. Check its "as of" date before quoting a balance.

## Always confirm before

- Any price sent to a client.
- Any purchase order to a vendor.
- Any figure that reconciles to a contract total.
- Any change to the numbering log.
