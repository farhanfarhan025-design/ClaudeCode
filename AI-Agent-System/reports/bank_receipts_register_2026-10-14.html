What this register can and cannot evidence. Sections 1 and 2 are complete: each line carries the cheque number or transfer reference, the bank and the date, and can be produced to a client, an auditor or a bank on demand. Section 3 cannot. QAR 280,000 is recorded as received and nothing on file says how it arrived — no cheque number, no transfer reference, and in four cases no date. The largest single payment the company has ever taken, the Oscar Prime 119,000 of 06-10-2026, does not even record whether it was a cheque or a transfer. Until it does, it is not known whether that money has cleared.
Receipts are missing on five of the six. Only RCT-279 was issued; the Oscar Prime 64,250 of August, the CCC 76,050, the Jollibee 27,600, the Samoosa 38,500, the Lean N Fit 17,000 and the Ruwais Farm 1,850 were all taken without a receipt leaving the office.
