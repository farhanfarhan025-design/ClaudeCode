  1. The Employer wishes to have constructed at its premises in the State of Qatar one (1) freezer room with an epoxy floor coating and one (1) preparation room, as more fully described in Schedule 1 (together, “the Works”).
  2. The Contractor carries on the business of the supply, fabrication, installation, testing and commissioning of cold rooms, refrigeration systems and commercial kitchen equipment, and has represented that it has the skill, experience, manpower and resources to execute the Works.
  3. The Contractor submitted its quotation reference QUT/DCTS/SQ118/2026 (Revised) dated 17 October 2026 in the sum of QAR 110,000.00 (“the Quotation”), and the Employer has approved and accepted that Quotation.

2.   SCOPE OF WORKS

  1. The Contractor shall supply, fabricate, deliver, install, test, commission and hand over the Works described in Schedule 1, complete in every respect, and shall provide all materials, labour, supervision, tools, plant, transportation, mobilisation and consumables necessary for their proper execution, save only for those matters expressly excluded by Schedule 3.
  2. The Works comprise, in summary: one (1) freezer room of 10.00 m × 9.50 m × 5.00 m operating at −18°C to −22°C in 100 mm PUF sandwich panel with a sliding door, a Dorin (Italy) AU2-H2201CS 22 HP air-cooled condensing unit and a Gunay (Turkey) GNE 350.6C2 unit cooler on R404A; a food-grade epoxy floor coating to 95.00 sqm of the freezer room floor; and one (1) preparation room of 9.50 m × 5.00 m × 3.00 m, unrefrigerated, in 50 mm PU sandwich panel with an aluminium door.
  3. Site survey. Before any fabrication is put in hand the Contractor shall carry out a site survey and confirm the final room dimensions with the Employer in writing. The dimensions stated in this Contract are the dimensions agreed at the date hereof; any change arising from that survey shall be dealt with as a Variation under clause 8.
  4. Equivalent equipment. The makes and models named in Schedule 1 are the equipment the Contractor has offered and priced. The Contractor shall not substitute any item of plant or equipment for another make, model or capacity without the Employer's prior written consent, which shall not be unreasonably withheld where the substitute is of equal or better specification and the Contract Price is not increased.
  5. The Contractor shall execute the Works in a good and workmanlike manner, with sound materials new and unused, in accordance with the manufacturers' instructions and with the applicable laws, regulations and municipal requirements of the State of Qatar.

  3. The Employer shall nominate the final positions of the condensing unit, the unit cooler and the control panel at the time of the site survey, and shall ensure that the nominated position for the condensing unit is adequately ventilated, accessible for maintenance and structurally adequate to carry it.

10.   THE EXISTING FLOOR — INSULATED FLOORING NOT INCLUDED

  1. The Parties expressly record that insulated flooring is not included in the Works to either room. Both rooms are to be erected on the Employer's existing concrete floor, and the epoxy coating described in Schedule 1 is a surface coating applied to that existing floor and is not an insulating layer.
  2. The Employer acknowledges that it has been advised that a freezer room operating continuously at −18°C to −22°C over an uninsulated slab may, over time, give rise to the freezing of the ground beneath the slab, to frost heave, cracking or movement of the slab, to condensation at the slab edges and to the loss of adhesion of a surface coating, and that the Works as priced contain no insulated floor construction, under-floor heating or under-floor ventilation to prevent it.
  3. The Contractor shall accordingly bear no liability whatsoever for frost heave, ground freezing, cracking, movement, settlement, condensation, or the lifting, blistering, debonding or failure of the epoxy coating, in each case to the extent arising from the absence of insulated flooring or from the condition, construction, moisture content, levelness or thermal behaviour of the Employer's existing floor. The warranty at clause 12 does not extend to any such matter.
  4. The Contractor shall, upon the site survey, report to the Employer in writing on the apparent suitability of the existing floor to receive the epoxy coating. If the Contractor advises that preparation beyond the cleaning, grinding and priming included in Schedule 1 is required, that additional preparation shall be a Variation under clause 8.
  5. The Contractor will, at the Employer's request, quote separately for an insulated floor construction to either or both rooms.

11.   TESTING, COMMISSIONING AND HANDOVER

  1. Upon completion of the installation the Contractor shall pressure test, evacuate, charge, run up and commission the refrigeration system, prove the controls, defrost and safeties, and pull the freezer room down to its design temperature.
  2. The Contractor shall give the Employer not less than two (2) working days' notice of the date of testing and commissioning, and the Employer shall attend or be represented.
  3. On satisfactory completion the Parties shall sign a Handover Certificate recording the date of handover, the readings taken and any outstanding minor items, together with operation training and the as-built documentation. The date of that certificate is the date from which the warranty at clause 12 runs.
  4. If the Employer takes the rooms into use, stores product in them or otherwise takes beneficial possession before the Handover Certificate is signed, handover shall be deemed to have taken place on the date of such use, and the final instalment shall thereupon fall due.
  5. Outstanding minor items recorded on the Handover Certificate shall be completed by the Contractor within fourteen (14) days and shall not be grounds for withholding the final instalment, provided they do not prevent the rooms from being used for their intended purpose.
